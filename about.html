Who's behind this

About us.

Draft Studio is built by a venture studio based in London — here's the thinking behind it.

Our role

We're an enabler, not just a builder.

Mission

Coalition, not competition

We build platforms that bring people together around a shared goal, rather than trying to do it all ourselves.

Approach

Start with students

Draft Studio — our student challenges platform — is the first thing we've built. It's a bet on what happens when students get real challenges, not simulations.

Belief

Enable, then get out of the way

Our job is to build the scaffolding — the challenges, the community, the structure — so good ideas and the people behind them can grow faster than they would alone.

Ambition

More than one platform

Draft Studio is the start. The studio behind it is built to keep launching things that help ventures and the people building them thrive.
