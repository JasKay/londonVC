Help it grow

Get involved.

Draft Studio grows city by city, campus by campus — and that mostly happens through students who help push it forward locally.

Ways to help

A few ways to get involved.

On campus

Be a campus rep

Help spread the word at your university and be the first point of contact for students there.

Community

Help run a challenge

Support the design or running of a challenge — from shaping the brief to being there on the day.

Word of mouth

Bring your circle

The best challenges happen with people who already know and push each other. Bring your friends in.

Local growth

Bringing Draft Studio to your city.

Draft Studio doesn't grow through a marketing budget — it grows because students bring it to their own campus and city.

First contact

Campus reps

The first point of contact at a university — the person others go to when they hear about it.

On the ground

Local challenge leads

Help shape and run a challenge in your own city, working directly with us.

Organic

Word of mouth

Does most of the work. Most people join because someone they know already has.

Want to help bring Draft Studio to your university or city? Tell us — we'll figure out the right role together.
